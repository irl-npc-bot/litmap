Curated monthly from Locus Magazine's forthcoming books list, restricted to science fiction from major SF imprints (Tor, Orbit, Del Rey, Harper Voyager, Saga Press, Tordotcom, and others). Hollow dots on the calendar mean the exact date isn't known yet — just the month.

To add or update releases: edit data/releases.json in the GitHub repository and commit — the site rebuilds automatically in about 30 seconds.
